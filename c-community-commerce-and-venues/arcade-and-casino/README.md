## How it runs itself

1. Players play the arcade machines, win tickets and trade them at the prize counter.
2. In the casino, players play slots, roulette and blackjack and cash out at the cashier.
3. Staff walk the floor and work the counters.

Everything runs on the server, so every player sees the same thing at the same time.

## Hooks for developers

- Players can take the attendant or clerk job.
- Plug in your own currency.

## Setup

1. Insert the model from the Creator Store.
2. Drag it into Workspace.
3. Press Play.

There is nothing else to set up.

## Clean, readable code

Every script is plain, readable Luau. Nothing is hidden or scrambled, and nothing is loaded

any script and see exactly what it does.
