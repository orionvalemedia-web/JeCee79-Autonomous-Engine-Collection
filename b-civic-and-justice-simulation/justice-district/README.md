## How it runs itself

1. Crimes create 911 calls and the dispatcher sends a unit.
2. Officers drive to the call, chase and arrest the suspect, and bring them in for booking.
3. Suspects go to jail and then to court, where a verdict is given.
4. Buildings you place in the world register themselves as places a 911 call can come from, with no code.

Everything runs on the server, so every player sees the same thing at the same time.

## Hooks for developers

- Players can take the jobs themselves: officer, dispatcher, jailer, judge and more.
- Hook your own pay to each job.
